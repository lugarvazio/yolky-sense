Noise

Attention is the scarcest resource in a home

Ordinary cameras detect motion the same way for everything. Working out what matters is a job that quietly gets pushed onto you.

01

One alarm for everything

A cat, a branch in the wind, a courier, a stranger — an ordinary camera reacts to motion the same way. Telling important from unimportant is left to you, every single time.

02

Trust fades fast

The first false alarms are annoying, the next ones get ignored. Within a week people simply turn notifications off — and the camera stays silent exactly when it should have spoken.

03

Watching isn’t knowing

The feed shows everything, but it doesn’t answer “what happened while I was away”. You have to dig through the archive to find out.

How it works

Not more data — less noise

Every part of the system exists to subtract noise, not to add it.

Learns to stay quiet

Remembers false alarms in specific places — a swing, tree shadows — and gradually stops reacting to them. The longer the system runs, the quieter it gets.

Weak spots — we show them ourselves
Night & dusk

At night the system nearly rests

Around 2% of findings happen at night. The hardest time is dusk: cameras switch to night mode and false alarms peak. A second-camera night check is already on for outdoor cameras; a dedicated dusk setting is in progress.

Cars

By position in frame — 38%

If the system names a car only by where it usually parks, it is right 63 times out of 168. By silhouette, accuracy is 75%. Postponed until more data builds up; we tested car make recognition with CLIP and shelved it: 52% against 72% for the silhouette.

Hardware

One box — 8 cameras

By October 3 the Mac Mini M1 was running at its limit. We took the load off: the engine gets 10 frames per second instead of every camera frame, decoding is hardware-accelerated, and the neural network runs only on motion. Latency on the gate camera dropped from 13–22 seconds to almost zero.

Where the numbers come from: the live database on the Mac Mini, a snapshot of October 3, 2026, the last day is incomplete. Accuracy is measured on findings a human reviewed, so it is a lower bound on the error. The full report is published without people’s names or animals’ nicknames.

Trust

Silence shouldn’t cost you trust

Staying quiet when all is well doesn’t mean we stop being honest with you about where your data is kept.

Processing on-site

Video and recognition are computed on your own hardware. By default nothing leaves the home. In our home, 8 cameras work this way on a single Mac Mini.

Remote access — tunnel only

An encrypted relay tunnel passes the connection through — it does not store video with us.

Your data is yours

You decide what to keep and what to delete. There is no middleman who sees the archive by default.
